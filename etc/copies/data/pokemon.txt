Outside of that career, he has hands-on personal experience across Pokemon live commerce, grading, vending, sourcing, and secondary-market analysis, and would love the chance to combine these two interests someday.

Pokemon TCG & Marketplace Experience (personal side interest, not his employer): Jeffrey built a Japanese Pokemon TCG live-commerce operation from the ground up as a side project, establishing direct supplier relationships and generating over 150K USD in revenue across six months of nightly 4 to 11 hour Whatnot streams specializing in Rip-Till-You-Hit and premium character-break formats. He also built and helped operate a Shopify-based Pokemon storefront, managing listings, inventory, pricing, fulfillment, and day-to-day e-commerce, as a personal venture rather than an employer. He has also built relationships across the Pokemon secondary market with suppliers, vendors, collectors, and high-volume repackers.

Pokemon domain knowledge: English and Japanese TCG sets, sealed product, raw cards, slabs, promos, and rarities; Whatnot live selling, Shopify, card-show vending, sourcing, fulfillment, and inventory management; sold comps, pricing trends, liquidity, population data, and market value; grading condition assessment, submission strategy, expected value, and slab valuation.
